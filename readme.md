## Case Studies

### 1. University Scholarship Allocation
Identifies eligible students based on marks, attendance, and family income.

### 2. Attendance Recovery Computation
Calculates student attendance percentage and the number of additional classes required to reach the target attendance.

### 3. Laboratory Chemical Inventory Validation
Checks chemical inventory quantities against predefined storage limits.

### 4. Campus Registration Identifier Analyzer
Analyzes student registration IDs and identifies details such as admission year, branch, and roll number.

### 5. Student Marks Matrix Analysis
Analyzes student marks, calculates totals and averages, and identifies top-performing students.

### 6. Food Waste Monitoring
Calculates food waste percentages and identifies whether food waste is within the acceptable limit.

### 7. Campus Resource Allocation
Allocates available computers to different activities based on priority and requirements.

### 8. Campus Festival Registration and Financial Audit
Checks participant registrations and calculates total fees, sponsorship, expenses, and final balance.

### 9. Multi-Stage ATM Transaction Audit
Processes ATM transactions such as deposits, withdrawals, and transfers while checking account balance.

### 10. Disaster Relief Supply Allocation
Allocates available relief supplies to different areas based on priority and demand.
